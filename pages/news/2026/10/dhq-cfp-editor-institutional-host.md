The deadline for expressions of interest for a new Editor in Chief and institutional host for <a href="https://dhq.digitalhumanities.org/index.html" target="_blank">Digital Humanities Quarterly (DHQ)</a> has been extended to January 8, 2027. The new Editor in Chief will serve a renewable five-year term, and collaborative arrangements involving multiple editors or institutions are welcome. The full call for proposals and submission guidelines are available [here](https://ach.org/news/2026/04/dhq-cfp/).

ACH and DHQ welcome inquiries. Please reach out to Julia Flanders (j.flanders@northeastern.edu), DHQ Editor in Chief, and Stefano Morello (smorello@gc.cuny.edu), ACH and DHQ Liaison.
