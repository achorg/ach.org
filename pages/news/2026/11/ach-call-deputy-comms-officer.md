Our Deputy Communications Officer collaborates with our Communications Officer to oversee all aspects of our communications, including keeping website content up-to-date (familiarity with GitHub a plus but not required), developing quarterly member newsletters, managing our social media accounts, and assisting in promoting major events (such as our annual conference). We seek individuals who enjoy this kind of outward-facing work, and would welcome a team of individuals who would like to work together. 

This is a four-year term: for the first two years, you'll serve as Deputy Communications Officer in partnership with the current Communications Officer (Winnie E. Pérez Martínez); the second two years, you'd become the full Communications Officer and partner with a new Communications Officer.

## Eligibility
You are very likely eligible! Our key needs are willingness to contribute effort, and enthusiasm for involvement with DH and the ACH—whether or not you’ve been involved in the past, or are keen to get started. We benefit from the perspective of folks new to DH or academia, just as much as folks who can bring past DH experience to the role. We hope for a set of applicants diverse as to race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, class, ability, profession (including students, folks in non-academic jobs), citizenship, nationality, and other identities and backgrounds. Membership to ACH before or upon appointment is required.
