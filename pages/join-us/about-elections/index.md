Elections are held each year for three new ACH Executive Council Representatives, whose terms will begin during the following summer. Additionally, every other year an election is held for Vice President(s)/President(s)-Elect.

The ballot for annual elections will open at some spring date, announced via email to people with current ACH memberships. Candidate biographies and statements will be posted on the ACH website and linked from that email.
