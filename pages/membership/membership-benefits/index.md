Membership in ACH is open to anyone with an interest in digital humanities. Benefits include:

* The satisfaction of supporting a robust professional community for DH scholars and practitioners, including allowing low membership costs increasing our accessibility
* A variety of benefits targeted especially to students (whose membership fees are a very affordable $26!) and junior scholars, including:
    * Eligibility for travel funding and bursaries at the annual ADHO DH and ACH conferences
    * Mentorship opportunities connecting you directly with friendly, experienced DHers in our community
    * DH conference newcomer dinners to welcome first-time conference attendees
* Ongoing discounts of up to 40% off of list price of DH books from university presses such as [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/), [University of Minnesota Press](http://www.upress.umn.edu/book-division/series/debates-in-the-digital-humanities), [and University of Michigan Press](https://www.press.umich.edu/browse/series/UM167)
* Eligibility for occasional support, as available, of conferences and other events relevant to the Digital Humanities, including the annual ACH Conference
* Significantly reduced registration fees at [conferences](http://adho.org/conference) sponsored by ADHO, the Alliance of Digital Humanities Organizations (as well as many other events)
* Opportunities to meet and work with a wide range of DH scholars and practitioners in our worldwide community
* The chance to get involved and make a difference in the research, scholarship, and working lives of digital humanists
* A vote in who leads and shape the ACH
