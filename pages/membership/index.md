The Association for Computers and the Humanities is your professional society for the digital humanities! Through our [activities](https://ach.org/activities), [conferences](https://ach.org/conferences), and [publications](https://ach.org/publications/), we support computer-assisted research, teaching, and software and content development in humanistic disciplines.

Founded in 1978, ACH provides a welcoming intellectual community and support for professional activities across the range of DH disciplines, practices, and cultures. As a member organization of [ADHO](https://adho.org/) (the Alliance of Digital Humanities Organizations), ACH connects the international DH community through conferences, discussion, and publications. Your ACH membership helps to fund student bursaries, open-access journal publication, mentoring services, development microgrants, and other important DH initiatives.

Further information regarding ACH membership is available throughout this site, including details about [membership benefits](https://members.ach.org/member-benefits/), our [join/renew form](https://members.ach.org/join/), and more. Please email us at membership@ach.org with any questions.
